How the Advanced Investment Calculator Works

The Enterprise Investment Calculator by Daily1Step uses high-precision compound interest mathematics with support for regular periodic contributions, annual salary step-ups, inflation adjustments, and tax considerations.

Formula & Mathematical Method

Future Value = P × (1 + r/n)^(n×t) + PMT × [ ((1 + r/n)^(n×t) - 1) / (r/n) ] × (1 + (r/n)×timing)

Where P is the initial principal, r is the annual nominal interest rate, n is the compounding frequency per year, t is total years, and PMT represents periodic deposits.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the "Annual Step-Up" feature?
Annual Step-Up automatically increases your monthly investment contributions each year by a set percentage (e.g. 5% or 10%). This accurately mimics career salary raises and significantly compounds long-term wealth.
What is the difference between Nominal Value and Purchasing Power?
Nominal value is the total face amount of money in the future. Purchasing power adjusts that number for annual inflation to tell you what that future balance will actually buy in today's real terms.
Is my personal or financial data private?
100% private. All calculations, chart projections, and CSV exports occur entirely within your local browser's memory without sending any financial figures to external servers.

Understanding Your Internet Speed & Network Health

Internet speed isn't just about raw download Megabits per second (Mbps). Quality of experience in online gaming, Zoom calls, and 4K streaming depends on multiple critical metrics:

Download Speed

How fast your device receives data from the web. 25 Mbps is sufficient for 4K streaming, while 100-500+ Mbps is optimal for households with multiple devices downloading games and movies.

Upload Speed

How quickly you send data out. Essential for video conferences, live streaming on YouTube/Twitch, cloud backups (Google Drive/iCloud), and uploading large videos.

Ping, Jitter & Bufferbloat

Ping measures reaction time (latency). Jitter measures fluctuation. Bufferbloat measures latency lag when the network is under heavy load. Below 20ms ping with low jitter is the gold standard for competitive gaming.

Recommended Speeds by Activity

  • Competitive Online Gaming (Valorant, CS:GO, Fortnite): 15-30 Mbps Download, < 25ms Ping, < 5ms Jitter.
  • 4K UHD & 8K HDR Streaming (Netflix, YouTube): 25-50 Mbps Download per stream.
  • Work From Home & 1080p Zoom Conferences: 15 Mbps Download, 5-10 Mbps Upload.
  • Smart Home with 15+ IoT Devices: 100-300+ Mbps Fiber connection.

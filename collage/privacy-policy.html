Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

1. 100% Client-Side Privacy Model

At Daily1Step Collage Tools (https://bypyay.github.io/collagetools/), we maintain a strict client-side architecture. All photo collage rendering, canvas transformations, and filters are executed directly in your web browser. Your photos are never uploaded to any remote server or stored in the cloud.

2. Google AdSense & Cookies

We may display advertisements served by Google AdSense to keep our service free. Google uses cookies (such as DoubleClick) to serve relevant ads based on browsing activity. You can opt out via Google Ads Settings.

3. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Because we do not collect, process, or store personal files or personal identifiers on remote servers, no user data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties.
